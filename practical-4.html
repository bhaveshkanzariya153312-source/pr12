Vision

To empower Innovators in Computer Science & Engineering through education, research, and ethical practices to create sustainable solutions with global impact.

Mission

1

Deliver quality education in Computer Science & Engineering through modern, technology-enabled pedagogies and strong industry collaboration.

2

Foster innovation, entrepreneurship, and interdisciplinary research to drive creativity and problem-solving.

3

Promote ethical computing, sustainability, and professional responsibility to nurture socially conscious graduates.

4

Prepare students for lifelong learning, global employability, and leadership in emerging domains such as AI, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and beyond.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

1

Design and develop secure, efficient, and scalable software systems, intelligent applications, and computing solutions using industry-standard tools and frameworks.

2

Apply Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and emerging technologies responsibly to create innovative and sustainable solutions for real-world challenges.
